Why Sponsor GUBER

  • Real proof, not impressions. Cash Drop winners photograph themselves claiming their reward and post it publicly — organic content your brand doesn't have to produce.

  • Ground-floor pricing. These are introductory rates for early sponsors — pricing rises as the user base scales.
  • Flexible commitment. Month-to-month, no long-term contract required to start.
All pricing above is an introductory proposal, not a fixed rate card — packages, minimums, and discounts are negotiable based on market, timing, and campaign scope. Final terms are confirmed in a written sponsorship agreement.
