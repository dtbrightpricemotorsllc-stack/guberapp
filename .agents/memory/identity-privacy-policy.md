---
name: Public identity privacy policy
description: Public identity rules for individual members, verified businesses, and private verification data.
---

**Rule:** Individual members are represented publicly by Guber ID, verification/reputation signals, completed activity, and relevant qualifications only. Personal names, usernames, handles, public profile photos, and avatar URLs stay private. Verified businesses may show their business name and logo.

**Why:** GUBER's product policy intentionally separates trustworthy participation signals from personally identifying information while preserving recognizable business identities.

**How to apply:** Treat every public or participant-facing response as an allowlist. Use a neutral GUBER avatar for individuals; keep names/photos available only for the owner, private verification, payments, or authorized admin workflows.
