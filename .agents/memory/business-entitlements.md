---
name: Business entitlement tiers
description: Durable rules for GUBER Business verification, subscription access, Founding Local Business, and PRO fees.
---

Paid business activity is available only when the business is verified and the plan is active or trialing. The free verified tier is limited to listing/discovery, profile, services/products, hours, and service area. BUSINESS+ and Founding Local Business add bookings/requests, payments/deposits, and customer history; BUSINESS PRO additionally adds enhanced storefront, priority promotion, and the lower platform fee. Founding Local Business never receives PRO benefits. Past-due plans fall back to free access.

**Why:** Verification alone must not unlock paid tools, and every server/UI surface must agree with the handout’s pricing promises.

**How to apply:** Resolve access through the centralized entitlement resolver for API guards, public business actions, navigation, dashboard modules, booking routes, and fee calculations. Keep ordinary marketplace and job flows outside these business subscription gates; keep purchasing and billing on guberapp.com.
