---
name: Day-1 OG limited-time campaign
description: Day-1 OG availability is governed by one time window, not a ZIP or city quota.
---

Day-1 OG founder availability is time-bound through the published campaign deadline, with no per-ZIP or per-city purchase cap. Members who qualify before the cutoff retain their Day-1 OG status permanently.

**Why:** The launch goal is to welcome as many early founders as possible during one honest, limited-time offer, rather than manufacture scarcity with location quotas.

**How to apply:** Keep all Day-1 OG purchase surfaces and checkout protection tied to the shared campaign deadline. Do not reintroduce “spots remaining,” “founder class closed,” founder-number, or ZIP-quota language unless the product owner explicitly changes the campaign model.
